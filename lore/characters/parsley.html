appearance

Parsley is a humanoid fish creature made of sentience mixed with fish DNA. He has white skin and occasionally purple scales on his body. He has purple scales, purple shorts, purple fins, and purple horns that fade to blue. Around his horns and arms, he can have water form a spiral around them sometimes. He has a big water tail behind him that stays afloat. His legs fade to fins, which are a deep purple color.


personality

He’s very reserved and quiet most of the time, He’s a bit shy and doesn’t like talking about specific things because of his past experiences. He doesn’t really understand much things other than the concept of living and death, and he just wants to survive considering what he’s been through. He’s very cautious about things and can come off as rude when in reality he’s scared.


trivia

  • He is a life guard to protect people from drowning and getting hurt from the water.
    • Due to his traumatic experiences in the labs, he always gets on edge whenever he sees people in water, similar to how he was, worrying that they may get injured.
  • Parsley does not have basic street knowledge.
  • Parsley met a celebrity before and didn't notice. Upon hearing this, he was still confused and acted like it wasn't a big deal.
