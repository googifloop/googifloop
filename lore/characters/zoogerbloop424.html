overview

zoogerbloop424 is a 12 year old gamer from the 2000s. His online identity is his ROBLOX avatar, with yellow skin, a green torso, black pants, and a sword on his head. He acts how you'd expect an average teenage boy to act during this era, what'd you expect?


appearance

Gabriel, aka zoogerbloop424’s ROBLOX avatar is a pale yellow skinned Robloxian, having a green torso, black pants, and a sword inside of his head. He has a fire ring on his torso.
Outside of ROBLOX however, he’s just your average kid. Tan-skinned, he sports usually a T-shirt along with some shorts and sometimes pants if the season is right or if he feels fancy. He doesn’t wear much, but he wears glasses due to his near-sightedness.


personality

Think about the most average teenage boy during that early 2010’s era. Around 2011-2012. He isn’t entirely unhinged, however. He simply wants to make it through life and make games when he gets older and also doesn’t mind “weird people” like furries, etc. what is usually deemed corny nowadays. He’s always optimistic and treats sadness like an obstacle, not a natural emotion. He’s gangster and totally awesome!!!
