overview

SW1TCH / Switch (Imani) is a TV Headed character. They are agender, but use masculine pronouns as well. He is mixed, being Jamaican, Filipino, and Chinese. He is a singer for a record label, and he has a passion with musical art.


appearance

SW1TCH is a dark-skinned person, with a TV as their head. They wear a blue jacket that is halfway zipped up, and has black leather pants on. He has mechanical/robotic feet.
His antennas on his TV head can change into any symbol, such as !, or ?, or even hearts. His screen is very expressive, so you'll know if something's up!
Formerly, before he had the TV head, he used to have thick dreads.


personality

SW1TCH has an overall melancholic temperament. He's a very thoughtful person, and thinks things out before they even begin! Being a vocalist, he pours his heart out into his passion, as well as into the people and things he loves. They're an empath, but they know when to put boundaries and strive for themselves.
