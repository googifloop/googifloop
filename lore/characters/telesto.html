overview

Telesto is an alien who has big green antennas and medium length green hair. He wears a white crop top, jacket, and blue jeans with blue shoes. He's an empathetic person and loves making friends.


appearance

Telesto is a green humanoid alien, with big green antennas coming out of his head from the top. He has green hair, with a small ahoge. He wears a beige zip up jacket that's unzipped, with a white crop top underneath it. He has blue jeans along with blue shoes with a white accent.


personality

He is a caring person with a big heart. He’s very clumsy but looks into details that people usually don’t notice. He’s very observant, looking and thinking about things beyond what people usually do or should. He acts silly but takes things seriously if he needs to. He isn’t quiet, being a bit louder than a regular person, but not entirely extroverted. He minds his business but isn’t afraid to make new connections!
