overview

Zay is a dicesona, using a D4 dice as the head. His outfit is dark, apart from his light gray tanktop, white socks and sneakers, and also his accessory, which is an unnamed neon teal drink. He's a caring person, but his logic isn't the brightest. Despite this, he still loves to make his friends happy.


appearance

Zay is an object head, with a D4 dice representing his head and his face on one of them. His skin is dark gray. He wears a light gray tank top and a black button up with a teal pattern reminiscent of old arcade floors. He has dark eucalyptus pants, with white socks and sneakers. Often, he has a glass beaker full of neon teal liquid in it with a white straw.


personality

He’s a bit dumb. That’s the first impression you get from him, a bit loopy. But if you even talk to him just for a second, you’ll already know he’s a caring person. He loves the people he’s friends with and goes lengths to see them satisfied. He’s very relaxed, and even if he’s stressed, he will still show little concern on his face. He’s not so expressive, but his actions and words say otherwise.
