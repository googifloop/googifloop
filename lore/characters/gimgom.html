overview

Gimgom is a zombie cat who used to be called Rock. Even before and after the accident, she still manages to be a cute little kitty.


appearance

Prior to the incident, Rock was your average British Shorthair kitty. Gray fur, big beady eyes, all that good stuff. They were the cutest thing ever!
Gimgom is a very shapely cat creature. They are mostly comprised of lines and have a simple shaped body which says G.G. That is up to interpretation. Nothing really to describe here other than the limbs look like twigs. They still are the cutest thing ever!


personality

It's a cat, what do you expect?
After the incident, Gimgom came back to life, but the sentience overridden their personality, and now acts more nonchalant and not as eager and exciting as before. I don’t think you need an MBTI for this.
