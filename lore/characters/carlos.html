overview

Carlos is a dude who's pretty swag. He's your average teen, and grew up to work at a terrible place, to where he became a victim of the wires and cogs. Now, he's a robot, but still is his old self, how it always was.


appearance

In his robot form, he’s an average humanoid robot. He has a blue amulet in his chest, which is pretty much his soul, and he can’t really do much about protecting it other than wearing clothes over it (It is very strong. It will NOT break unless you have like 100 trucks ram into him.) He has black joints and abdomen; he also has robot antennae on both sides of his head (usually drawn as one for simplicity since he’s usually facing one way). He has a satellite dish on top of his head that points up and left relative to his face.

Prior to being turned into a robot, he looked similar to Gabriel, except a bit older. He had medium length black hair, and he usually wore headphones every day, even if teachers got mad at him, he’d still wear them. He either wore jackets or T-shirts with a long sleeve under them. He wore jeans or joggers. Most of his clothes are thrifted.


personality

Carlos is your average teenager back then, being a bit reckless and rowdy. However, despite his nature he’s a caring person at heart. He loves his brother deeply and cares a lot about him and likes giving him opportunities he usually wouldn’t get. Sometimes, he even would do crazy things just to make sure he’s happy or for the sake of his friends, like trespassing onto school grounds afterhours to get a plushie for a friend over the break. He’s not the most extroverted person; however, he has a decent social standing. He’s a bit outwards with his feelings, but sometimes if things get too awkward/quiet, he settles down. Most of the time, he’s a bit loud. Even though his voice speaks levels, his actions speak even more, especially with the people he loves.
