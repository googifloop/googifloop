appearance

Tuberculosis is a teal person with no hair at all. He uses a red train conductor hat with a black brim, with a smiley face pin on the left (his right). He wears a silk white long sleeve shirt with purple Bengal stripes on it, wearing a black vest atop it. He also wears a necktie under the vest. He wears a gold name lapel on his vest, although it says his initials only. He wears gray pants and has a darker gray belt and black shoes. He is a bit nonchalant, and decently put together and composed.


personality

He’s a bit nonchalant but cares enough to notice if something’s up. He’s usually carefree and doesn’t struggle with many things that he does good at. He’s not usually curious and prefers to stay to a limited circle of things that he indulges in (Not like he isn’t open minded). He sees a lot of things as therapeutic and thinks of an overall picture rather than small things at a time, which can make him slip up occasionally. He’s overall decently put together, even in a really basic go to casual fit to run to the store. Even without trying, his outfit can look good, he can look all composed even when inside his battery runs low.
