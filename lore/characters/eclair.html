overview

Eclair is a ROBLOXian with a tiara. Heavily based off of Noli, she has some of his features, but is quite the opposite. She is much like a plant, and her swaying in the wind as she photosynthesizes matches her personality, being kind and dainty!


appearance

Eclair is a Robloxian with a crystal green tiara, diamond in the middle with two protruding tetrahedrons curving up and towards the sides, with dots at the end. A heart of the same material can be seen above the diamond in the middle. They have a half toned body, with one side being black/gray and the other being sea green. A heart shape in the middle of her torso divides his body into the colors, and a white face mask covers the black/gray part. A flower is seen in replacement of blush on the green part of its face.


personality

Dainty person who loves to be kind and spread happiness and fight for what is right! She doesn’t really do much harm unless it’s harming someone who’s malicious and has malevolent intent. He occasionally slips things up and is a bit clumsy but not too much to the point where they’re helpless.
