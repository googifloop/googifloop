overview

Blake is a short and bouncy person who has a reserved yet energetic personality. He is small, has gray skin, medium length gray hair with long sideburns, wears a fiord colored shirt, with a pants of a darker shade, black shoes, and occasionally a blanket with star patterns on it.


appearance

Blake is a small guy who is yay high. Did I mention that he’s small? He has gray skin and gray medium length hair. He has sideburns that reach to his shoulders and has a prosthetic leg. He wears a Fiord colored shirt, with pants of a darker shade. He wears black shoes and sometimes wears a blanket with a blue and yellow starry pattern on it as a cloak.


personality

He’s bouncy, energetic, yet reserved and can either contribute to an ongoing state of flow in a party or be that calming peace that resolves a conflict and keeps things well. He is usually very open to new concepts and isn’t afraid to be a bit experimental, especially with his music. He’s full of fun and whimsy and enjoys things ranging from size to size. It’s like he can switch from coffee to an energy drink if given the right criteria.
