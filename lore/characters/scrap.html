overview

Scrap is a cold and off-putting individual whose only purpose is to be Scruffy's replacement. Created out of spite and vengance, he is poorly constructed from materials akin to Scruffy, and yet Scrap still looks off from him.


appearance

A loose recreation of Scruffy. White skin and hair, hair is really simplistic and doesn’t have much layers or much to it in general. Wears clothing with dulled colors compared to Scruffy, has a golden dog tag and wears a regular T-Shirt instead of a polo. He also wears shorts instead, and has no shoes.


personality

Hand crafted out of spite and one goal; to replace Scruffy and to spread his cause. He pretends to be Scruffy, mimicking his every act and even his voice. He pretends to be all cheery and talkative like him, and is a bit indistinguishable. Truthfully, he’s cold and calculates his every move in order to manipulate those closest to Scruffy. Inspect close enough, and you’ll see that everything is a bit off. The way his jokes are awkwardly timed and such... "What kind of awesome of this?" one that shouldnt be trusted
