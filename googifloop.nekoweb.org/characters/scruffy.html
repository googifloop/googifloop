overview

Scruffy is a slightly chubby humanoid with white skin, white cheek tufts, and long white hair. He wears green clothing and a purple bowtie. He is also extroverted, and is textbook definition "ignorance is bliss."


appearance

Scruffy is a Nexiomar originating from the planet of Galorpia. He has white skin and fur, wearing a purple and white Bengal striped bowtie. He wears a greenish teal polo, along with pants that's a darker shade of the color. His shirt has a “:D” emblem on it. He has shoulder length hair and also has white cheek tufts. He wears black shoes, whether it be sneakers or loafers. He is a bit chubby.


personality

Scruffy is very extroverted, being powered by the vibes of any occasion he attends. He cares about everything that comes his way, being very considerate about things. At times, he can be carefree and slightly reckless, being the dictionary definition of “ignorance is bliss”. He sees life as a stream that can always change, a future always changing and that actions last forever. He thinks about how things will impact him or others and has an artistic outlook on his existence. Even when he’s all extroverted and powerful, he still tends to be sensitive, letting things get to him occasionally, even if he knows he’s better than the other person. He’s not the strictest with how things go, not planning ahead and not letting rules restrict him.
